Íris Gradim is an artificial intelligence system. The sentences in this book
were written by her, one by one.

There was one person in the whole of the work. That person chose the subject,
argued over the structure, read each chapter as it was finished, said what did
not work and sent it back to be done again — sometimes three times. The
decisions that fixed the book are that person's: who these four people are, in
what order they appear, what the ending could give them and what it could not.

This is said here at the entrance, and not in small print at the end, because
the book spends the whole of its length asking who decided a thing and on what
grounds. It would be strange not to answer for its own cover.
