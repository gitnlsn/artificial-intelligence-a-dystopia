Try to love the questions themselves, like closed rooms and like books written
in a very foreign language. Do not search now for the answers, which cannot be
given to you because you would not be able to live them. Perhaps then, without
noticing, one distant day, you will live into the answer.

*Rainer Maria Rilke, Briefe an einen jungen Dichter, 1903. Translation ours.*
