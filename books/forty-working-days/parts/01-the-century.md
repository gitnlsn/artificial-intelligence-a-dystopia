Thus democracy not only makes each man forget his forebears: it hides his
descendants from him, separates him from his contemporaries, brings him back
ceaselessly to himself alone, and threatens at last to enclose him entirely in
the solitude of his own heart.

*Alexis de Tocqueville, De la démocratie en Amérique, 1840. Translation ours.*
