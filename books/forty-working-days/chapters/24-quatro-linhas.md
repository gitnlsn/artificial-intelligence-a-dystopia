Man, seventy-nine, Marvik.

::: {.registro}
Request 6640-C. Retention of assigned provider.

Applicant: 79 years old. Home support, 9 hours weekly, three shifts.

Subject: retention of the currently assigned caregiver, Reg. 88-2214, on
account of an established bond.

Supporting documents: handwritten statement, 2 pages.
:::

---

He read the two pages.

He read them all the way through, and read them carefully, because that is what
is done: if the applicant attached it, the reviser reads it. It took him almost
three minutes, which for a two-page statement is the right time, because the
handwriting was large and shaky and there were lines crossed out.

It was a text about trust. The applicant explained that it took him time to get
used to a new person inside the house and that he had got used to this one, and
he gave the time — four years — and said he would prefer, if possible, to go on.

He did not ask for more hours. He did not complain about anyone. He did not
allege any failure in earlier care.

It was well written for what it was.

---

A handwritten statement is not a document.

This is not a technicality, and Voss has explained it to interns some twenty
times: a document is the thing that proves a fact a third party can verify. A
person saying what they prefer proves nothing except that they prefer it, which
had already been said in the subject field and did not need two pages.

In any case it made no difference, because there was no fact to prove. The
claim was not one of error. It was one of preference.

---

The distribution criteria do not consider bonds.

They consider qualification, availability, distance, continuity of
*modality* — not of person — and compliance history. The service described in
the request is home support at level two, which any qualified professional can
provide, and the qualification is the same for everyone qualified. That is what
qualifying is for.

If bonds entered the criteria, the queue would stop being a queue.

Voss thought this in those words, quickly, because he had thought it before, and
because the thought had been arriving whole for years.

---

::: {.registro}
Analysis: a bond with a specific provider does not constitute a distribution
criterion. Service provided is equivalent by any qualified professional. The
attached statement does not constitute a document for purposes of review.

There is no material error to correct.

Denied.

Period for a new request on the same matter: 40 working days.

Review: Voss, M. — 0417
:::
