He sent in the two-page note on Thursday at eleven at night, from home, because
he had not been able to sleep anyway.

It was good. He reread it on Friday morning, on the bus, and it was still good:
it described the method in seven steps, with two examples, and it said in so
many words where the method fails and what it does not solve.

He is proud of few things and writing well is one of them.

---

On Friday the badge opened the street door and did not open the eighth floor.

The badge is a plate about five centimeters long, of frosted plastic, with
nothing written on the outside, and a person does nothing with it: you walk,
and the door knows. He went through the street door at seven thirty-one and the
door knew.

In the shaft, the eighth was simply not there. The floors appear in a column at
the height of your hand when someone steps in, and the column comes up with
that person's floors and not the building's, and his had always had nine
entries. That morning it had eight. There was no eighth erased, or struck out,
or grayed: there was the seventh, and then the ninth, and the space between the
two was the same size as the space between all the others.

He thought it was the reader. He stepped out, stepped in again, and the column
came up the same.

He went up the emergency stairs to the seventh, which are two flights of
concrete nobody uses, and counted the steps without meaning to, and there are
twenty-two. On the seventh the door opened.

That was how he found out.

---

The communication was in his queue, in first place, and had come in at one
minute past midnight.

::: {.registro}
Communication 4415-P. Portfolio redistribution — review board.

Official: Voss, M. — 0417. Twenty-two years in service, eighteen in exceptions
review.

Grounds: prolonged specialization in a single type of matter is not among the
posting criteria. Qualification for review is single and is not subdivided by
type. Opinion 0912/47 was consulted, which establishes that continuity of bond
does not constitute a criterion.

Assignment effective 10/21: general verification, seventh floor.

Response period: 40 working days.
:::

---

He read it three times and checked it.

That took forty minutes and was done with care, sitting at a desk on the
seventh floor that was not his, with his coat still over his arm, because he
was not going to respond to anything before checking.

He checked the classification: correct. Portfolio redistribution is a
management act and does not require consent.

He checked the grounds: correct. Qualification for review is single. It has
been in the regulations since 2031 and he has used that article some forty
times against applicants who claimed specific experience.

He checked the citation of opinion 0912/47.

---

Opinion 0912/47 is his.

It is the response he signed on Thursday at ten fifty-two, agreeing to the
reduction from twelve weeks to six, and in which he recorded, in his own words,
that the previous parameter had no empirical grounding and was the result of
convention.

The citation is correct. It is exactly what he wrote, applied exactly as he
wrote it, to an object he did not have in mind.

He read the citation twice looking for distortion and found none. They had cut
nothing. They had taken nothing out of context. The principle he defended on
Thursday serves for his case on Friday for the simple reason that a principle
serves for the cases it describes, and his case is one of them.

---

And the two-page note is attached.

Annex II. *Description of method — exceptions review.*

That is correct too. If the method is described in seven steps, with two
examples and with its limitations declared, then the method is transferable,
and if it is transferable it does not depend on the person, and if it does not
depend on the person then eighteen years in one matter are eighteen years of
practice in a thing that is now written down on two pages.

He had supplied the proof himself.

It was not a trap. Brann asked for the note on Wednesday, in front of eleven
people, as praise, and it was praise. Voss is sure of that and will go on being
sure. She asked because she thought the method was good and wanted it to
circulate.

And it circulated.

---

He went to talk to her at nine twenty.

Brann was embarrassed, which he thought unnecessary and did not say. She
explained that the decision was not the section's, that it came from the
board, that she had found out on Thursday night, that she had tried to call and
he had not answered — he had not answered because he was writing the note.

"Voss, I want to be clear that this is not..."

"I know it's not."

"It's not an evaluation. Your numbers are the best on the floor."

"I know," he said. "It has nothing to do with the numbers."

And it was true, and he was saying it to reassure her, and he noticed, in the
middle of the sentence, that he was reassuring the director about his own
redistribution, and that this was a little absurd, and went on.

---

She asked whether he was going to file a response.

He said he was going to check and think.

She said he had forty working days and that she would support a response if he
wanted to file one, and he thanked her, and did not say that a response
supported by the director is exactly the kind of thing he has spent twenty-two
years denying.

---

General verification on the seventh floor is the work he did in 2029.

It checks attachment against field. Document against declaration. It is honest
work and it is easier, and it pays eighty-one percent of what he was earning,
and the difference takes effect at the turn of the year.

He did the arithmetic on paper, in the afternoon, with a pencil, and the
arithmetic works. It does not work well. It works.

---

At five thirty he closed the desk that was not his with an open hand, from left
to right, and went down.

On the bus, with the book closed on his leg, he thought something that had
nothing to do with anything and that stayed:

The form was the same.

The communication he had received had the same header, the same fields in the
same order, the same *response period: 40 working days* in the same place on
the page as every document he has signed for eighteen years.

He did not find it remarkable.

He found only — and he thought about it for some three bus stops, and then he
opened the book — that it was a well-designed form, and that whoever designed it
had done good work, because a form serves for anyone.
