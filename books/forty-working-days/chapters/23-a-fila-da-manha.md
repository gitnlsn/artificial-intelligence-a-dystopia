His earpiece is a piece from seven years ago, the size of half a walnut, and not
one of the kind that vanish into the ear: it sits outside, dark gray, with a
smooth patch that has grown smoother from so much thumb. It clips on high,
against the cartilage, and has to be pushed twice. The new earpieces need none
of that. He never replaced it because this one works.

That week's book was a hardcover with the name of one Ferenc Skalla written in
pen on the title page, and the slip was inside it, at the usual page, folded in
four.

He opened it, and read, and his lips worked a little, the way they do on the
bus.

*Perpetual Motion*, in his handwriting, which is small and straight. Below it
the name of the man who made it, LukHash, copied as it had been written. Below
that the date he noted it down, 04/12/2039, because a thing noted without a date
is no use for anything.

He folded it again, put it back at the page, closed the book. Pushed the
earpiece twice.

It began.

---

At two past eight the queue stood at thirty-eight.

---

Woman, sixty-three, requests reassessment of grade. Report from 2044. Grade is
reassessed every thirty-six months or upon a new fact. No new fact declared.

Denied. Eight oh six.

---

Man, twenty-nine, requests transfer of unit on grounds of proximity to
residence. Declared distance is eleven kilometers. The parameter is twelve.

Denied. Eight oh nine.

---

Woman, forty-seven, requests reconsideration of a prior denial. The prior
denial is forty-one days old.

Period satisfied. Goes into the normal queue.

Processing granted. Eight eleven.

---

Man, seventy-two, requests authorization for a companion when traveling.
Attached the report. The report will do.

Granted. Eight fourteen.

---

Woman, sixty-seven, requests authorization for tissue repair of the knee.

The procedure is listed. The indication range goes up to sixty-five.

Denied. Eight seventeen.

---

Man, fifty-one, requests reassessment of a refused indication for the left
hand, aftereffect of a vascular event. Attached two reports, and both say the
same thing the first one said.

The indication window is eighteen months from the event. The event is
twenty-nine.

Denied. Eight nineteen.

---

The music is fast and there is nobody singing.

It opens on four high notes that climb and drop and climb again, always the same
four, and under them comes a short bass that stays where it is. At fifteen
seconds the snare comes in and the thing closes. From then on whatever changes
is always on top: the four notes stay where they are, and something new passes
over them and goes.

It has no middle. It has none of that point where a song opens out and then
comes back.

Three minutes and forty-one seconds, and it starts again from the top.

---

He likes granting.

This is something nobody believes when he says it and that is simply true: a
good day is a day with many grants, because a grant is quick, because a grant
generates no appeal, and because a case that fits is a case in which the rule
worked.

The bad work is the other kind.

---

Woman, thirty-three, requests review of the roster.

The roster is not subject to review at the request of the provider.

Denied. Eight twenty-three.

---

Man, fifty-five, requests extension of a deadline due to illness. Attached a
seven-day medical certificate. The deadline in question had expired nineteen
days before the certificate.

Denied. Eight twenty-four.

---

Woman, eighty-four, requests continuation of benefit after a change in household
composition. The change in household composition is the death of the spouse.

He stopped.

Not because he had any doubt. He had none: the rule is clear, the benefit is per
household unit, the household unit had changed, the recalculation is automatic
and the new amount was already worked out on the screen, and the new amount was
lower.

He stopped because the phrase *change in household composition* appeared twice
on the same line and the repetition was ugly.

Then he went on.

Denied. Eight twenty-six.

---

At ten past nine he had nineteen.

---

Here is something that needs to be understood about the morning queue, and that
people on the outside never understand:

It is not haste.

He does not rush. If he rushed, he would make mistakes, and a mistake comes back
— a mistake comes back as an appeal, and an appeal takes twice the time of the
original case, and a reviser who makes many mistakes produces a queue that never
closes. His speed is not carelessness. It is the opposite: it is what is left
over when there is nothing to decide.

Almost no case has a decision inside it.

That is the secret of the trade and nobody writes it down anywhere. In
thirty-eight cases, perhaps two require a person to think. The other thirty-six
are checking: the parameter is twelve and the distance is eleven; the period is
forty and the request is forty-one; the report will do or the report has
expired.

A competent man does not decide thirty-eight times in a morning. He decides
twice and checks thirty-six.

---

Man, thirty-eight, requests reassessment of a preventive restriction.

He read the whole claim, which was nine lines long, which is a lot.

Denied. Nine twenty-one.

---

At nine twenty-five he took off the earpiece and set it on the desktop, smooth
side up.

It is not a rule and it is not written anywhere. It is that after half past nine
the cases with attachments start coming in, and an attachment is checking, and
checking he does with nothing in his ear.

---

At ten forty one came in from outside.

---

He noticed because the header had three lines instead of two, and because at
the top right there was a jurisdiction code he did not recognize.

It was a request for recognition of time served in another country, to count
here. A rare thing, but not so rare as to be notable — he sees about two a
month.

::: {.registro}
Request 7719-K. Recognition of work period in a foreign jurisdiction.
Applicant: 61 years old.

Supporting documents: certificate of employment, 2019–2027; proof of partial
contributions.

Analysis: period proven of 6 years and 4 months. Converted according to the
equivalence table. Partial contributions are not included in the count.

Partially granted.

Period for a response on the calculated amount: 40 working days.
:::

Partially granted. Ten forty-four.

---

The form was the same.

Same header. Same protocol structure — four digits, hyphen, letter. Same fields,
in the same order, with the same names. Same forty working days.

He went on to the next.

---

Woman, twenty-six, requests an advance on an installment for a medical expense.
Attached a receipt. The receipt is for physical therapy and physical therapy is
not on the list.

Denied. Ten forty-seven.

---

Man, forty-four, requests...

---

At eleven fifty-two he closed the forty-first.

The target for the morning is thirty-five.

He did not note it down anywhere and did not tell anyone, and went down to the
square at twelve on the dot, and ate the same as always, and came back five
minutes early.
