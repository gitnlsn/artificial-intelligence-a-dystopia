She has been putting this song on since Tuesday.

It isn't her favorite. Her favorite is a different one and changes almost every
week. This one is for after her bath, turned down low, when she's already in her
pajamas and there's nothing else to do until tomorrow.

It came up on Tuesday the way things come up. She knows how it came up. There's
nothing special about that.

She let it play to the end because she couldn't be bothered to change it, and
then she put it on again.

---

"*space in the space*."

She says the whole name and the music comes.

---

The words aren't in her language.

She found that out on Tuesday too, almost at the end, and thought it was funny,
and kept listening. The girl sings something Nina doesn't understand and sings it
nicely. The middle part goes up. And there's a place almost at the end where
everything stops, half a second, before it comes back.

She already knows where the place is. She waits for the place.

---

On Wednesday she read what was written.

She could read all of it, from beginning to end, and she read it carefully,
sitting down, without doing anything else at the same time.

It wasn't hard. Each sentence made sense.

Together they didn't.

She read it again and it still didn't, and then she gave up and left just the
music, which was nicer.

---

The light in her room goes down by itself at ten and she was still awake.

She went to the living room. Her mother had both hands full and was crossing to
the other side.

"Mom."

"Hi."

"You know Uru?"

"Yeah."

"I listen to this song and I still don't understand it."

"One day you'll understand."

Her mother said it without stopping walking.

---

Nina went back to her room.

She spent a while thinking about how a person understands a thing later, if they
didn't understand it now, when it's the same thing and the person is the same
person.

She couldn't find the way. But there must be one, because her mother said it
very sure.

---

She put it on again.

And at the place almost at the end, right when everything stops, she stopped too.
